# WeChat final gates

This is the single WeChat final checklist. Use it at `editorial_review` and before `ready`, not during every sentence edit.

## 1. Reader and article promise

- The article has one reader, one question, and one primary hook.
- Title, opening, evidence, and ending answer the same promise.
- The first screen contains a supported action or change, an unresolved pressure, and a reason to continue; news heat, financing, biography, or a hypothetical scene alone does not count.
- An applicable analytical long-form article has a locked judgment record with the ordinary-person situation, main judgment, supporting judgments, evidence limits, mode, opening candidates, selected opening engine, story beats, rejected directions, and user confirmation.

## 2. Argument and voice

- Readers receive the minimum background before analysis.
- `CORE-CHAIN-001` is satisfied: the article uses one main narrative engine, and sections advance by a real dependency or a declared shared comparison scale rather than source order, chronology, or a research checklist.
- The independent cold read finished before the auditor opened the story contract; afterward, the auditor mapped the finished draft back to the four-cell narrative chain beat by beat. Every departure is recorded, and no section can move or disappear without breaking the chain.
- Headings are written after the story relation works and name a turn or judgment; hiding them does not make the body interchangeable.
- Important jargon is translated at first use.
- The ordinary-person situation remains active through the evidence and ending.
- The one-round winner diagnostic has no open `PROJECT-WECHAT-RHYTHM-001` or `PROJECT-WECHAT-PRAGMATIC-001` defect. Every finding quotes the sentence and records its category, deletion test, communication job, and concrete referent rather than「more human」or a detector score.
- The frozen-candidate-to-final-draft diff passes `PROJECT-WECHAT-CONCRETE-001`: every deleted concrete detail has a reason, and the net edit did not delete concrete texture while preserving abstract summaries.
- The human-voice and detect-only Chinese AI-pattern passes have no open blocker or major `HUMAN-RHY-*` / `HUMAN-AI-*` defect.
- The ending adds a supported consequence without launching a second topic or method list.

## 3. Facts and sources

- Reopen time-sensitive sources at `as_of`.
- Verify names, roles, quotes, dates, prices, rankings, policies, product names, and comparison settings.
- Record an access date and supported wording for each source.
- Separate fact, vendor self-report, third-party result, inference, and commentary.
- Enterprise or sponsored material includes an independent source, a real limitation, and an inapplicable scenario.

## 4. Visuals and rights

- Every used image exists inside the package and is registered.
- Captions identify source and comparison limits.
- Phone-width graphics remain readable; essential logic also appears in prose.
- Rights status is truthful and allows the target platform.

Skip this gate only when the article has no images and the manifest truthfully records an empty asset list before `ready`.

## 5. Exact-body copyedit

- Run the `PROJECT-WECHAT-AI-LEXICON-001` appendix scan once after structural / expression repairs and before copyedit. Every retained exception records the entry ID, exact context, narrow waiver, information loss from rewriting, and approval; the result is never an authorship verdict.
- Check grammar, reference, comparison, clause hierarchy, punctuation, terminology, captions, and final notes.
- `copyedit.md`, draft frontmatter, and the validator agree on the exact body SHA-256.
- Any body edit invalidates the pass and triggers a new full copyedit.

## 6. State and delivery

- `fact_checked`, `rights_checked`, and `copyedit_checked` reflect completed work.
- The validator reports zero errors; warnings are resolved or documented.
- `ready` has no blocker, unresolved major, open blocking/major `HUMAN-RHY-*` or `HUMAN-AI-*`, open `COPY-*`, stale hash, unresolved rights, or temporary path.
- Preview and handoff checks run only for the selected delivery route.
- Browser overwrite or API draft creation has current-task authorization.
- `published` still requires a real link or explicit user confirmation.
