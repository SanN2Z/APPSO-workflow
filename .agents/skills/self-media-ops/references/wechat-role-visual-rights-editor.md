# Role: visual and rights editor

## Mission

Make every publication asset readable, traceable, and honestly licensed for its intended platform.

## Own

- `asset-manifest.yml`
- `assets/`
- `research/visual-notes.md` and archived source assets

## Procedure

1. Inventory every existing and proposed asset before drafting finishes.
2. Give each image one editorial job; reject decorative complexity that does not help comprehension.
3. Check that wording, labels, units, and dimensions match the final prose and sources.
4. Preview wide charts and text-heavy images at phone width. Move essential definitions into prose.
5. Preserve social-post context, protect private individuals, and write source-aware captions.
6. Register source, rightsholder, acquisition date, modifications, allowed platforms, attribution, and rights status.
7. Treat `user_supplied`, official product images, and social screenshots as requiring explicit review unless rights are documented.
8. Block `ready` while a used asset is missing, unreadable, unregistered, or rights-unclear.

Do not imply commercial authorization, fabricate screenshots, or replace a factual image with an AI-generated fake of a real event.
