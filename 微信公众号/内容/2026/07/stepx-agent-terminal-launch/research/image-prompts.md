# Image2 生图提示词

## 图 1：公众号封面

用途：微信公众号封面，建议画布 2.35:1，最终裁切到 900×383。

Prompt：

> Wide editorial technology illustration for a Chinese WeChat article, 2.35:1 aspect ratio. A dark graphite stage with a single luminous orange path shaped like a decisive forward step, passing through three abstract layers: a neural model lattice, an operating-system permission grid, and a handheld device silhouette. At the end of the path is a small warm orange agent light, suggesting an AI that moves from thinking to acting. Premium magazine art direction, restrained black, charcoal and vivid orange palette, cinematic depth, crisp geometry, generous negative space on the left for later title placement. No people, no real product photo, no company logo, no fake interface, no Chinese characters, no text, no watermark.

图注：`图：AI 生成的编辑示意。画面表达“模型—系统—终端”的执行链，不是 STEPX Neo 的产品图或真实界面。`

## 图 2：Agent 闭环解释图

用途：正文解释图，建议画布 3:2，至少 1200px 宽。

Prompt：

> Clean editorial infographic illustration, 3:2 aspect ratio, dark background with vivid orange and warm white. Show one continuous closed loop with four clearly differentiated visual zones but no text: sensing through camera and microphone symbols, remembering through a compact memory lattice, deciding through branching paths converging into one choice, and acting through authorized app cards and a device control. Add a visible human handoff gate before a high-risk action, plus a rollback arrow returning to a safe state. Minimal, elegant, high contrast, smartphone-readable, precise vector-like shapes, technology magazine style. No real logos, no brand marks, no fake screenshots, no Chinese characters, no text, no watermark.

图注：`图：AI 生成的编辑示意。一个能办事的手机 Agent，不只要感知、记忆、决策和行动，还要在高风险动作前让用户确认，并在出错时能够停下或恢复。`
