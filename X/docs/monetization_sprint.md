# 7-Day X Monetization Sprint

Last updated: 2026-07-04

## Goal

Make the first monetization signal as fast as possible:

- first qualified replies;
- first DMs;
- first call bookings;
- first paid pilot.

Do not rely on X creator revenue sharing. Treat X as a distribution and lead
generation channel.

## Reference From `snail`

The purchased `snail` skill is useful as a content packaging layer, not as a
business model by itself.

Reusable ideas:

- titles/hooks should sell the click, not summarize the content;
- every post must answer "is this about me?" and "is this useful to me?";
- generate many hook variants, then filter by identity fit, emotional trigger,
  information gap, counter-intuition, platform fit, and title/body alignment;
- adapt the same idea differently for X, LinkedIn, WeChat, and Xiaohongshu;
- use platform-native language instead of generic summaries.

Do not copy the full skill into this repo. Use it as inspiration for our own
hook scoring and content review workflow.

## 7-Day Execution

### Day 1: Positioning

- Pick one narrow audience.
- Write one sentence: "I help [audience] get [result] using [method]."
- Rewrite profile bio and pinned post.
- Prepare a simple intake form or manual DM checklist.

### Day 2: Proof Assets

- Create one public example using a fake or own account:
  - before/after profile;
  - 10 weak hooks -> 10 stronger hooks;
  - sample weekly content calendar.
- Publish 3 posts showing the transformation.

### Day 3: Lead List

- Build a manual list of 50 target accounts.
- Prioritize people who:
  - have a product/service;
  - post inconsistently;
  - have weak hooks;
  - already care about distribution.
- Leave 10-20 useful public replies. Do not spam DMs.

### Day 4: Offer Posts

Publish 3 offer-adjacent posts:

- pain post: "Your product is good, but your X account makes it look dead."
- teardown post: "I rewrote 5 weak X hooks for AI tools."
- direct offer: "I am taking 3 pilot clients for a 7-day X launch sprint."

### Day 5: Free Mini-Audits

- Offer 5 public mini-audits.
- Keep each audit useful but incomplete:
  - one positioning issue;
  - one hook rewrite;
  - one next action.
- Route interested people to DM.

### Day 6: Close Pilot

DM flow:

1. "What are you selling?"
2. "Who is the buyer?"
3. "What have you tried on X?"
4. "Do you want a 7-day launch sprint or just a hook rewrite pack?"

Close with a small fixed-price pilot. Avoid custom scope.

### Day 7: Deliver And Turn Into Content

- Deliver the pilot.
- Ask for permission to anonymize before/after.
- Publish a case study without private data.
- Raise the next pilot price.

## Hook Scoring

Score every post idea from 1-5:

- identity fit: does the reader see themselves?
- pain clarity: is the problem concrete?
- usefulness: is there a promised outcome?
- curiosity: is there an information gap?
- credibility: can we prove or demonstrate it?
- CTA fit: does it connect to the offer?

Ship posts with total score >= 22/30. Rewrite the rest.

## Product Roadmap For This Repo

Build only what helps sell or deliver the service:

1. content idea database;
2. hook scorer;
3. draft queue;
4. safe posting script with explicit confirmation;
5. lead tracker;
6. weekly report generator;
7. client deliverable exporter.

Avoid building broad automation before the first paid pilot.

## Safety

- No fake engagement.
- No mass DM.
- No automated replies to strangers.
- No follow/unfollow growth hacks.
- No scraping behind login.
- No posting without explicit confirmation.

Fast monetization should come from a clear offer and useful public proof, not
from platform manipulation.
